# Neuroplasticity

Neuroplasticity is the brain's ability to reconfigure its neural connections throughout life. This discovery has profound implications for leadership, learning, and change.

## Key Findings

- Neurons that fire together wire together (Hebbian principle)
- The brain can form new connections and strengthen existing ones at any age
- Learning physically changes the brain's structure
- When we learn something new, we create new neural pathways
- The brain's capacity for change is essentially unlimited

## Implications for Leadership

- Employees can always learn new skills and develop new ways of thinking
- Change is always possible — no one is "wired that way" permanently
- Leadership's role is to create conditions that foster new neural connections
- Repetition and emotional engagement strengthen new pathways
- [[hard-wiring]] explains how new patterns become automatic over time

## Practical Applications

- Provide frequent, specific feedback to strengthen desired neural patterns
- Create safe environments for experimentation and learning
- Use the [[create-model]] to guide people through creating new thinking
- Follow up with the [[feeling-model]] to ensure new patterns persist
