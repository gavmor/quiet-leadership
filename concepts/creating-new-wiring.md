## Core Concept

Contrary to earlier scientific beliefs, the brain can create new neural connections throughout life. Neuroplasticity research shows that learning, new experiences, and even reading a book physically restructure the brain. This provides a foundation for hope: people can always learn and grow, regardless of their starting point or age.

## Key Principles

- **Neuroplasticity**: The brain's ability to form new neural connections at any age
- **Learning = physical change**: Every learning experience alters neural structure
- **Emotional engagement helps**: Emotionally significant learning creates stronger new pathways
- **Repetition builds strength**: Repeated practice reinforces new neural connections

## Implications for Leadership

- **Growth mindset foundation**: Everyone has capacity for new learning
- **Create learning environments**: Design experiences that foster new neural pathways
- **Emphasize novelty**: New challenges stimulate more growth than routine tasks
- **Support emotional engagement**: Learning is more effective when emotionally engaging
