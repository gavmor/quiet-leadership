# CREATE Model

A six-step framework for generating new thinking when facing challenges, developed by David Rock. This model helps leaders and individuals move from vague concerns to concrete insights and actionable commitments.

## The Six Steps

**C - Clarify the question**
Transform the problem into a question that invites creative solutions. Instead of "What's wrong with our sales?" ask "What can we do to improve our sales next quarter?" Questions focus the mind on possibilities rather than problems.

**R - Reflect**
Pause to think about the question, especially when facing challenges. Reflection allows insight to emerge naturally and helps avoid getting stuck on old, familiar ideas.

**E - Explore possibilities**
Brainstorm creative solutions rather than settling for conventional answers. Explore different ideas and see what might be possible.

**A - Articulate insights**
Express your insights verbally and listen to how others express theirs. Speaking your insights helps others understand them and potentially build on them.

**T - Try actions**
Commit to specific, concrete actions that will bring insights to life. Without commitment to action, thinking remains merely an intellectual exercise.

**E - Evaluate and learn**
Reflect on the consequences of actions and continue the cycle of creating new thinking. The evaluation feeds back into the beginning, creating an ongoing loop of improvement.

## When to Use

Use the CREATE model when:
- Someone is worried or struggling to see their way forward
- The same problems keep recurring without new solutions
- You need to generate creative options rather than settle for conventional ones
- You want to help someone commit to actionable steps

## Connection to Neuroscience

The CREATE model works with the brain's natural tendency to generate new neural pathways when facing unfamiliar questions. By clarifying the question and exploring possibilities, you stimulate the creation of new associations and ideas that weren't previously accessible.
