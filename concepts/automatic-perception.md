## Core Concept

Our perception of reality is largely automatic, shaped by our existing neural connections. We don't see the world objectively; we see it through the lens of our established mental maps. This makes perception feel immediate and real, even though it's filtered through our individual wiring.

## Key Principles

- **Perception is predictive**: The brain predicts what we'll experience based on past learning
- **Filtered reality**: Each person experiences a different "reality" based on their neural maps
- **Hard-wiring drives perception**: Frequently used neural pathways shape what we notice
- **Emotional charge matters**: Emotionally significant events create stronger neural pathways

## Implications for Leadership

- **People experience different realities**: Team members may genuinely see events differently
- **Don't assume shared perception**: What's obvious to one person may be invisible to another
- **Explore perceptions**: Ask questions to understand others' mental maps
- **Respect subjectivity**: Avoid dismissing others' perceptions as "wrong"
