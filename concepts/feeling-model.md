# FEELING Model

A seven-step framework for following up after new thinking has been created, ensuring that insights and commitments lead to lasting behavioral change. Based on the neuroscience principle that "cells that fire together, wire together" — repeated experience strengthens neural pathways and creates lasting change.

## The Seven Steps

**F - Find out how they're doing**
Check in with the person to discover what they've been trying and what the results have been. Listen to their experiences without judgment.

**E - Encourage action**
Help them continue to take actions based on their new thinking. Provide support for the difficult steps they need to take.

**E - Evaluate progress**
Assess whether the new approach is working. Help them understand the results they're getting and what this means for their thinking.

**L - Look for new ideas**
Help them find new ways to act that are consistent with their new thinking. Explore additional applications of their insights.

**I - Inspire and energize**
Reinforce their enthusiasm for the new approach and help them maintain motivation. Recognition of progress is key here.

**N - Never give up**
Understand that change takes time and repetition. Continue to support them even when progress seems slow.

**G - Guide them back to their own insights**
Help them return to their own understanding and wisdom. The goal is for them to sustain their own learning, not become dependent on your guidance.

## Why Follow-Up Matters

Without follow-up, the new thinking created through the CREATE model often doesn't become lasting behavior. The brain requires repetition of the new neural pathways to solidify them. Follow-up provides this repetition and support.

## Connection to Neuroscience

The FEELING model leverages the brain's plasticity — its ability to change and grow new connections. Each time someone acts on their new thinking, the neural pathway for that behavior becomes stronger. Follow-up helps ensure this pathway is exercised repeatedly until it becomes the default response.
