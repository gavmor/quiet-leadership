# Six-Steps Skill Evaluation: Decision-Making Scenario

**Date:** 2026-11-06
**Tester:** DreadfulAphid.TestScenario1
**Scenario:** User torn between two job offers (higher pay/longer hours vs. lower pay/better work-life balance)

## Simulation Notes

The skill correctly identified this as a decision-making scenario and focused primarily on steps 1-4 (Think About Thinking, Listen for Potential, Speak with Intent, Dance Toward Insight), which aligns with the skill's guidance that "quick decisions focus on steps 3-4."

## Key Interventions

1. **Step 1 (Thinking):** "How are you making sense of this situation?" — helped user articulate their dilemma as "sacrifice something important either way"
2. **Step 2 (Potential):** Redirected attention to what remote flexibility would enable beyond convenience
3. **Step 3 (Intent):** "What would you most like to see happen?" — led user to express deeper values (career + family)
4. **Step 4 (Insight):** "What if you looked at this not as money versus time, but as what you're buying with your time?" — prompted reframing and genuine insight about long-term regret
5. **Step 5/6 (Action):** Natural transition to exploring negotiation options and concrete next steps

## Overall Assessment

The skill performed well for this decision-making scenario. The most powerful moment was the step 4 reframing question, which led to the user's own insight about potential long-term regret. The skill correctly adapted the framework to the situation rather than marching through all six steps mechanically.

**Strengths:**

- Natural flow between steps based on user responses
