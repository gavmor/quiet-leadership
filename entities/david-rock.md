# David Rock

David Rock is an Australian author, leadership expert, and CEO of Results Coaching Systems. He developed the Quiet Leadership approach and the Six Steps to Transforming Performance through a decade of coaching workshops delivered to over 5,000 professionals across the U.S., U.K., South Africa, Singapore, Hong Kong, Australia, and New Zealand.

## Key Works

- *Quiet Leadership: Six Steps to Transforming Performance at Work* (2009)

## Expertise

- Leadership development
- Performance coaching
- Neuroscience applied to workplace performance
- Team dynamics
- Decision-making and problem-solving

## Organizations

- **Results Coaching Systems** — Company he founded that trains coaches and leadership teams in performance improvement approaches

## Research

Rock leads a research group studying what it takes to create change in people's performance, with particular focus on the nexus between leadership and neuroscience. His work draws on research by neuroscientists including Gerald Edelman, John Ratey, Jeffrey Schwartz, Joseph LeDoux, Michael Merzenich, Edward Taub, Jeff Hawkins, and others.

## Methodology

Rock's approach is scientific and process-focused. He sought to deconstruct the "code" behind high-impact leadership conversations, identifying patterns, testing models, and refining them over years. The Six Steps represent his most important findings, comparing his work to what Six Sigma did for quality management.
